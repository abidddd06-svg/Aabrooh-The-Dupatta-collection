Our Story

The Meaning Behind AABROOH

Aabrooh — a word rooted in grace, dignity and quiet honour. It is the feeling we chase with every dupatta we create: something that doesn't just complete an outfit, but carries the wearer with a little more poise through her day.

We started AABROOH with a simple frustration — that "premium" dupattas were either impossibly expensive or, when affordable, felt mass-produced and forgettable. We wanted something in between: boutique-level fabric, finishing and design, priced for real, everyday women.

Elegance, considered

Every AABROOH piece begins with fabric first. We work with organza, silk, chiffon, georgette and Banarasi weaves — chosen for how they fall, not just how they photograph. Borders are finished by hand wherever the design calls for it, and every new collection is tested against one question: would we wear this ourselves, on the days that matter?

Built for every chapter

From a plain ivory drape for a Tuesday at work, to a hand-embroidered piece for a wedding mandap — AABROOH is designed to move with you across everyday wear, festive occasions, weddings, parties, Eid and gifting. Not just a dupatta. A statement of grace, in whichever room you wear it.

Where we're headed

We're a small, growing brand based out of Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, shipping pan India. Every order is packed by hand, and every message — on WhatsApp, Instagram or email — is read by someone who actually cares whether your dupatta arrived the way you imagined it.
